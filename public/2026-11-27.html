原文摘要节选：Knowledge-intensive video generation remains challenging because prompts often omit the facts, appearances, and temporal relationships required for accurate generation. While search-based workflows can retrieve missing evidence, manually designed strategies limit adaptive planning, and conventional training suffers from costly video-level feedback and coarse trajectory-level credit assignment. In this paper, we present Loupe, a video generation agent that retrieves and verifies textual and visual evidence to construct evidence-grounded temporal video prompts with reference images. We further introduce Rubric-Based Credit (RBC), which leverages prompt-specific factual, visual, and temporal requirements to evaluate intermediate search and planning decisions before video rendering.

However, existing comparisons between supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning vary many factors simultaneously, making the contribution of rollout policy difficult to isolate. We study the effect of rollout policy in a controlled strong-to-weak distillation setting, by independently varying rollout policy, token-level KL direction, and learning rate across the Llama3 and Qwen2.5 model families and reasoning tasks spanning scientific, medical, and arithmetic domains.
